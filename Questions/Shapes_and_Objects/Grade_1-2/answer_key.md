# Shapes & Objects - Grade 1 - 2 - Answer Key

1. What does RECTANGLE plus RECTANGLE equal? Count all the sides.
   **Answer:** 8 sides (4 + 4)

2. What does HEXAGON plus HEXAGON equal? Count all the sides.
   **Answer:** 12 sides (6 + 6)

3. What do THREE triangles equal? Count all the sides.
   **Answer:** 9 sides (3 + 3 + 3)

4. What does PENTAGON plus SQUARE equal? Count all the sides.
   **Answer:** 9 sides (5 + 4)

5. Which real-life object looks like a CIRCLE?
   **Answer:** A) Clock

6. A stop sign has what shape?
   **Answer:** B) Octagon (8 sides)

7. Count the shapes. How many triangles are there?
   **Answer:** 5

8. How many corners does a rectangle have?
   **Answer:** 4

9. Which shape has 6 sides?
   **Answer:** C) Hexagon

10. A slice of pizza looks like which shape?
   **Answer:** A) Triangle
