# Multiplication - Kindergarten - Grade 2 - Answer Key

1. How many ducks are there in all?
   **Answer:** 4

2. How many stars are there in all?
   **Answer:** 6

3. Add the groups: 2 + 2 + 2. How many flowers in all?
   **Answer:** 6

4. How many cherries are there in all?
   **Answer:** 6

5. A car has 4 wheels. How many wheels do 2 cars have?
   **Answer:** 8

6. One hand has 5 fingers. How many fingers on 2 hands?
   **Answer:** 10

7. Count by 2s. What number is missing?
   **Answer:** 8

8. 4 kids each have 2 balls. How many balls in all?
   **Answer:** 8

9. Which picture shows 2 groups of 3 stars?
   **Answer:** A

10. 3 plates have 1 apple on each plate. How many apples in all?
   **Answer:** 3
