# Multiplication - Grade 1 - 2 - Answer Key

1. How many apples in all? Write the multiplication sentence.
   **Answer:** 3 x 4 = 12

2. Write a multiplication sentence for this array.
   **Answer:** 3 x 5 = 15 (or 5 x 3 = 15)

3. Write 4 + 4 + 4 as a multiplication sentence and solve it.
   **Answer:** 3 x 4 = 12

4. A bee has 6 legs. How many legs do 3 bees have?
   **Answer:** 18

5. A car has 4 wheels. How many wheels do 5 cars have?
   **Answer:** 20

6. Write TWO multiplication sentences for this array.
   **Answer:** 2 x 4 = 8 and 4 x 2 = 8

7. What is 5 x 3? Use the picture to help.
   **Answer:** 15

8. Grandma makes 3 sandwiches for each of her 4 grandchildren. How many sandwiches?
   **Answer:** 12

9. Fill in the missing number. Use the array to help.
   **Answer:** 2

10. Skip count by 5s. What are the two missing numbers?
   **Answer:** 15 and 25
