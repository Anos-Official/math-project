# Data Sense - Grade 1 - 2 - Answer Key

1. Count the fruit. How many apples are there?
   **Answer:** 6

2. Which fruit is there the MOST of?
   **Answer:** B) Pear (7)

3. Each flower stands for 2 flowers. How many daisies are there?
   **Answer:** 10

4. Each car stands for 5 cars washed. How many cars were washed on Tuesday?
   **Answer:** 35

5. Read the tally marks. How many votes did Red get?
   **Answer:** 13

6. Look at the bar graph. How many days were rainy?
   **Answer:** 10

7. Look at the bar graph. How many MORE cats than fish are there?
   **Answer:** 5 (9 - 4)

8. This table shows how many kids have each item. Which item do the FEWEST kids have?
   **Answer:** C) Pencil boxes (24)

9. Which letter goes in the MIDDLE of the Venn diagram (a fruit that is also red)?
   **Answer:** C) Apple

10. Each fruit stands for 1 fruit. How many fruits are there in all?
   **Answer:** 19
